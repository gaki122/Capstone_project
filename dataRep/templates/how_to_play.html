🎮 How to Play Data Quest

Learn data representation, answer questions, earn points and stars, and improve your knowledge through different levels.

How Does Data Quest Work?

Follow these simple steps to begin your learning journey.

1

📖 Learn

Start with the Learn section to understand important concepts about data representation. You can study ASCII, text, images, and audio before playing the games.

2

🎮 Play

Start a Data Quest level and answer interactive questions. Read each question carefully and choose the best answer.

3

⭐ Earn

Correct answers give you points and stars. Try to improve your score and complete each level successfully.

4

📊 Track

Students who join a teacher's class can track their points, stars, completed levels, attempts, and progress from their dashboard.

5

🚀 Improve

You can replay levels and try to improve your previous scores. Practice helps you understand the concepts better.

6

🏆 Master

Complete all available levels and build strong knowledge of digital data representation.

Choose How You Play

Data Quest supports different types of players.

👤

Guest Player

You can start Level 1 without creating an account. Your result is private to your current game session and is not visible to teachers.

🎓

Student

Create a student account and enter the join code provided by your teacher. Your scores and progress can then be tracked by your teacher.

👨‍🏫

Teacher

Teachers can create multiple sections, give students a class join code, and monitor the learning progress of students in their own sections.

⭐ Understand Your Rewards

🎯

Points

Earn points by answering questions correctly.

⭐

Stars

Earn stars based on your level performance.

🔥

Best Streak

Try to maintain a strong streak of correct answers.
