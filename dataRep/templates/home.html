About Data Quest

Data Quest is an interactive learning platform designed to help students understand how different types of information are represented digitally. Through learning activities, games, challenges, points, stars, and achievements, students can learn concepts in a more engaging and enjoyable way. Teachers can create and manage multiple sections, manage their students, and monitor student learning progress through the teacher dashboard.
