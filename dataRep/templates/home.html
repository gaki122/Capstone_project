About Data Quest

Data Quest is an interactive educational platform designed to help students understand digital number systems through learning, practice, and game-based challenges. Students can begin playing without an account or create an account to keep their own progress. Students can also join a teacher's class using a class join code. Teachers can create and manage their own classes and monitor the progress of students who have joined their classes.
